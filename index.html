After pressing Save, the "How rating works" cards, share images and every player's rating are updated: the difference between the old and new rules is applied to the current tournament and to all finished tournaments in History. Own goals from finished tournaments are not stored, so a change to the own-goal value only affects the current tournament and future ones.
🗂 Backups (drafts)
Open the Admin tab to load backups…
A backup of the previous data is made automatically when you save (at most once every 20 minutes) and always before players or history are deleted. The newest 15 backups are kept. "Restore" brings back everything from that moment: players, ratings, teams, matches, history and rules. The current data is backed up first, so a restore can be undone.
